OVERVIEW

Dandy is a digital dental lab platform that provides dentists with crowns, bridges, implants, clear aligners, retainers, dentures, and night guards through a modern digital workflow.

The platform combines intraoral scanners, cloud-based case management software, and advanced dental lab manufacturing to help dental practices streamline restorative and orthodontic workflows.

Dentists capture digital impressions, submit cases through Dandy software, collaborate with lab technicians, and receive finished restorations manufactured through Dandy's digital lab infrastructure.
